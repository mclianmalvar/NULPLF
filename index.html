      Questions

      Who posts the items?

      Only Security Office staff. They log in with a staff account to post the items turned in at the office, approve claims and release items. Students can search, claim and report, but can't post or change items.

      How do I prove an item is mine?

      When you claim, describe something not shown in the post: a sticker, what's inside, a scratch, the lock screen. At pickup, show your NU ID. Staff release the item only when the details match.

      Why can't I see the full details of an item?

      Posts leave out identifying details on purpose, so only the real owner can describe them. Photos of IDs and wallets are blurred before they're posted, and IDs show the name but not the student number.

      What happens to my personal details?

      Only Security Office staff see your name, student number, contact details and proof of ownership, and only to process your claim. They're deleted automatically 30 days after your claim is closed, following the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Your sign-in details and lost-item reports stay on your own phone; they're sent to the office only when you claim an item.

      Why do I need to sign in?

      So the Security Office knows who is claiming and how to reach you, without you typing your details every time. In this prototype you enter your name and student number once. The full version would use your NU email login instead.

      How long are items kept?

      Items are kept for 30 days. After that, staff mark them for disposal or donation, so check early.

      I found something. What do I do?

      Bring it to the Security Office. Staff will tag it and post it here the same day.

      Will I get notified?

      File a report and check My requests. Possible matches appear there as soon as a similar item is posted.
